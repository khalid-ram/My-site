## Overview

EduSmart is an EdTech marketplace connecting students with qualified teachers and educational centers across Egypt. It started as a **single-teacher academy**. As a freelance project manager, I led it from concept to a **delivery-ready MVP**, turning it into a scalable marketplace.

## Discovery & Research

I ran a **competitive analysis** of the EdTech market to understand the landscape and find opportunities for differentiation.

## The Solution

- A **three-sided marketplace** connecting students, teachers, and educational centers.
- An **admin dashboard**. I owned its structure and design, building interactive prototypes so decisions could be made faster and with clearer visuals.

## Execution

- **Requirements → prototypes.** I worked with the business team to turn requirements into visual prototypes in Figma, which kept every stakeholder aligned on what was being built.
- **Async-friendly communication.** I planned meetings and recorded walkthrough videos so the team could communicate faster and more clearly.
- **Design quality.** I followed up closely with the designer to make sure delivered solutions met the agreed requirements.
- **Delivery and handover.** I owned delivery, testing, and handover to the client for a smooth, high-quality transition.
