Validation surfaced pains for students (commuting time, high costs, fixed schedules, missed lessons, hard to compare teachers, little progress tracking), teachers (limited reach, nowhere to showcase content, manual student and subscription work) and educational centers (manual registration and code distribution, weak tracking of code sales, heavy offline admin, hard to scale). The opportunity: rebuild CSA Academy around school years and teachers."
