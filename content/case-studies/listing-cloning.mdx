      alt: "Understanding the process: we sat with the compound team, mapped how a new compound is added, and traced where brokers' data comes from. A developer launches a compound and shares the same data with a broker group; Aqarmap's compound team publishes it once, clean and verified, while every other broker re-types the same data, 10–15 minutes each. Insight: the data brokers receive is the same data we already publish, so let brokers clone it. Brokers post near-identical units that differ only in view, area, floor, and price."

Problems: publishing took 10–15 minutes per listing, brokers re-entered duplicate data shared by developers, and one unit had many listings of uneven quality. Solutions: clone compound description and photos in one tap, pick a live unit to clone up to 12 fields, and keep every step skippable and editable. Result: from 10–15 minutes to 2–3 minutes, about 5× faster."
